# ADR 0001: RHEL 10 as the base distribution

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-28

## Context

LocumView needs a base operating system for both the reference desktop and, eventually, the production desktop image. The project targets regulated environments (healthcare, life sciences) where compliance evidence and vendor accountability matter, and it is a portfolio project aimed at Red Hat as a target employer.

## Decision

Standardize LocumView on RHEL 10 as the base OS.

Reasoning: RHEL is the dominant enterprise Linux distribution in regulated industries. It has published DISA STIG and CIS baselines, a FIPS-validated crypto module, Red Hat's security response process, an available support contract, and a ten-year lifecycle. It is also the target employer's product.

## Alternatives considered

- **SUSE (SLES)**: similar compliance pedigree, strong presence in some hospital and European settings. A reasonable alternative but does not serve the portfolio's Red Hat alignment goal.
- **Ubuntu LTS**: has commercial support and certifications, but weaker regulated-industry compliance pedigree than RHEL or SLES.
- **Rocky Linux / AlmaLinux**: RHEL-compatible without a subscription, excellent for labs and cost-free experimentation. A supported enterprise distribution wins over these where compliance evidence and vendor accountability are the actual selling point of the project, which is the case here.

## Consequences

The distribution is the most swappable layer in the stack, since provisioning is Terraform plus Ansible. The Ansible roles can branch to support SLES or Ubuntu later with manageable effort if the project's scope changes. LocumView is standardized on RHEL for its compliance and support story, not architecturally locked to it.
